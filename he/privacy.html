Rock Rise — Privacy Policy

Published by SO SUN · Last updated 22 July 2026

Rock Rise is a single-player band-management game. We built it to be private by default. This policy explains exactly what the game does and does not collect, and how to control it.

What we collect

If you opt in to sharing data, Rock Rise collects anonymous gameplay analytics to help us find bugs and improve the game:

  • which screens you open and how long you spend on them;
  • gameplay events — gigs played and their results, songs written, tiers reached, purchases of in-game items;
  • crash and error reports;
  • a randomly generated device install id and a short, non-identifying player code;
  • your chosen band name and music genre, and the app build version.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or coarse location, or advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Where your data goes

Anonymous analytics are processed by PostHog on servers in the European Union. Your game save stays on your device (in the app's local storage); we do not upload it. If a future version offers optional account sign-in or cloud backup, that will be clearly opt-in and this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

  • Turn analytics off anytime in Settings → “Share anonymous data.” When off, nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Reset your data by wiping your save from the Band screen.
  • Request deletion of any data tied to your device by emailing us your player code (shown on the feedback screen).

Some builds distributed for testing may have analytics enabled by default so we can diagnose issues; testers are informed of this, and the Settings control still applies in the public release.

Children

Rock Rise is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app.
